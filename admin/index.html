Publishing checklist

Before public launch, configure D1 binding DB, apply SQL migrations, and set Cloudflare secrets ADMIN_EMAIL, ADMIN_PASSWORD, and SESSION_SECRET. Use a long unique password and keep secrets out of GitHub.
  • Student-facing content appears only after publishing.
  • Mathematical notation is rendered by KaTeX when students open the article.
  • Student PDF downloads include a diagonal mathslearn.online watermark.
  • Keep a D1 backup/export and test restore before relying on the platform.
  • Use original or licensed question papers and images.
