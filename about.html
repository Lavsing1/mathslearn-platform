About MathsLearn

MathsLearn is a free learning platform built to make mathematics easier to understand for school and competitive-exam students. We organize notes, solved examples, formula sheets and question papers by class, board and subject so students can find exactly what they need, quickly.

What we offer

  • Class-wise and board-wise study notes
  • Step-by-step solved examples
  • Quick-reference formula sheets
  • Previous-year and sample question papers
  • A free online books library
